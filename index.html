03 · Zero-ad plan

Codes, a free week on television, and organic work.

Paid ads stay at zero. Coupons improve conversion of traffic that already exists; they do not replace acquisition. Email-list size and site traffic are unknown, so volume is not invented here.

fraimous.com

Hard-coded codes, one per order

Codes are written into the site. Stripe coupons are not used. MEMBER25 remains 25% off a first member purchase on the main site. 5N0W84LL3FFCT remains 46% off The Pair (formerly The Set) on Snow Globe only — do not extend it to the main site for BFCM. Proposed, not live: one or two new percent-off codes around 15% for 27–30 November, a small change that still needs a yes. Checkout is one-time payment only, so a free week of membership on the site is not possible by 26 November.

Frame TV app

Where a free week can live

A free pack, or a free-week unlock, fits Fire TV Live App Testing. Pack price is still an unapproved $29 placeholder; the offer is access, not a priced sale. Samsung remains waiting on a yes, and a store review started this late is unlikely to clear by 27 November. A later Stripe subscription would be required if a site-side free week is wanted after BFCM.

Organic

Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok

Post from @definitelystephany and the existing social pack. Draft captions and pins; do not boost. Email and SMS are drafts only until a yes — list size is unknown. A gift with purchase, if used, should be digital (near-zero cost of goods), not a free physical print. Gift cards should not be promised unless the site already supports them; that is more than a small code change.

Held or draft only

Amazon, Etsy, hotels

Amazon prints stay on hold. There is no Etsy plan and no shop. B2B hotel or restaurant outreach can be a one-pager in draft; nothing is sent without a yes. Licensing cycles are slower than a seventeen-day window.

06 · Risks and blockers

What stands in the way.

Binding

Checkout is on staging

Four fixes need a yes to ship, then a QA recheck. If they stay on staging, BFCM week stays at $0, codes or no codes.

Structural

No site subscriptions

A free week of membership on fraimous.com is not possible by 26 November. That offer belongs on the television app, or after a later Stripe subscription build.

Demand

No ads, unknown traffic

Codes do not create visitors. Traffic, email-list size, and social reach are not measured in this report. Volume is therefore conservative and labeled as an estimate.

Channels

Amazon hold, no Etsy

Amazon prints remain on hold. There is no Etsy shop and no Etsy plan. Neither is in the 2026 weekend number.

Television

Fire TV testing, Samsung waits

Pack price is an unapproved placeholder. A free week can be tested; it should not be forecast as cash. Samsung is not a 2026 BFCM storefront unless a yes was already given in time for review — it has not been.

Margin

Deep cuts and stacking

One code per order. Do not put the 46% Snow Globe code on the main site. Exclude extra-large canvas from percent-off codes. MEMBER25 already gives members 25% — a 15% public code stays beneath it.
